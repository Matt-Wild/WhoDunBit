# WhoDunBit

**Purpose:** Introduce WhoDunBit and provide links to project documentation.  
**Updated:** 14 October 2026  
**Related documents:** [Documentation index](Documentation/README.md)

WhoDunBit is a standalone multiplayer social deduction FPS inspired by **Trouble in Terrorist Town** and **Blood on the Clocktower**. Players must uncover supernatural enemies hiding among them through investigation, bluffing and gunplay.

Bystanders and trusted Enforcers face Monsters and their human collaborators, Minions. Monsters and Minions have functional **Human Forms**, giving them genuine Bystander role abilities: a Monster posing as a Medic can actually heal other players. Demonstrating a role's abilities is therefore no guarantee of innocence.

The game combines map exploration, equipment gathering and hidden-role interactions with supernatural transformations and identity theft. Deaths are not automatically announced, leaving players to discover bodies, investigate missing people and piece together conflicting accounts. Monsters choose when to risk their cover for supernatural power, while coordinated humans work to expose and eliminate them.

## Development direction

The project is in pre-production, targeting Windows PC and Steam using Unity and C#. Initial multiplayer testing will use Unity Services for friend-hosted, host-authoritative play with hotjoining. Steam integration and dedicated servers are planned for later stages, alongside extensive host configuration and community modding.
